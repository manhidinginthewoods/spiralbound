Game Guide
Combat
Combat starts in manual mode — pick a spell from your hand each round. Clear the Training Grounds (W1) to unlock auto-combat. Boss fights are always manual. XP comes from kills; level up to learn new spells.
Your Deck
In the Spellbook tab, set how many copies of each spell go in your deck. You draw a hand each round and can only cast what you draw. More copies = more likely to draw it. Deck size grows as you rank up.
Priority Rules
Priority rules control auto-combat. Set IF/THEN rules like "IF no blade → cast Blade" or "IF sigils above 3 → cast your strongest spell." Rules are checked top-to-bottom each round.
Gear & Gold
Buy gear from the Shop to boost stats. The Bazaar has reagents, potions, snacks, and seeds at fluctuating prices. Equip wand cores and woods for bonus stats as you find them.
Side Activities
Familiar — hatched from boss drops, boosts your stats, can be sent on expeditions
Garden — grow reagents and snacks from seeds (unlocks W2)
Workshop — craft potions, enchantments, jewels, and gear
Fishing — catch fish for gold, complete the Fish Tome
Challenges
The Spire — tower challenge with a restricted deck (unlocks W3)
Dueling Club — 1v1 fights vs named NPCs including your rival (unlocks W1)
Professor's Assignments — rotating goals for bonus rewards
Bestiary — track enemies, extract animus, craft treasure cards
The Journey
Fight through 8 worlds, defeat bosses, rank up, and graduate. Enroll in a new school for a fresh run — mastery auras from each graduation stack permanently. Graduate all 6 schools to unlock Balance and enter The Spiral, the infinite endgame.
