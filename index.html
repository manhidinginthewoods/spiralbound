Stormcallers deal devastating damage but have the lowest health and their spells fizzle more than any other school. When a storm spell lands, the fight is usually over.

Fire
Pyromancers specialize in damage that burns over time. Their spells keep dealing damage round after round, and some even heal the caster while burning the enemy.
Pyromancer · Prof. Ashveil
Ice
Frostbinders have the highest health and resistance in the game. Their damage is lower, but they can absorb hits and build up power the longer a fight lasts.
Frostbinder · Prof. Rimward
Life
Verdancers have the most reliable spells of any school and powerful healing magic. When they heal more than they need, the excess turns into damage.
Verdancer · Prof. Fernsby
Death
Wraiths steal life from their enemies. Many of their attacks heal the caster for a portion of the damage dealt, making them incredibly hard to take down.
Wraith · Prof. Marrowick
Myth
Fabulists summon minions to fight alongside them and take hits in their place. They also have spells that hit multiple times, punching through enemy shields.
